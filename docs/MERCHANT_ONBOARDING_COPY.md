# Merchant onboarding — approved draft copy

## Headline
Get found for catering orders you might otherwise miss.

## Subhead
Join Never86'd's free owner network. Tell us what your kitchen can actually serve, and our catering agents can match qualified group-food requests to your restaurant. You stay in control of menus, capacity and whether to accept an order. Checkout is handled by an approved ordering partner, not Never86'd.

## CTA
Apply to join — free owner seat

## Form
Restaurant name; business location; contact name and email; contact phone (optional); service ZIPs; pickup/delivery; minimum notice; minimum order; max guests; menu URL or upload; dietary options; current ordering links (Maho Cater, DoorDash, Uber Eats, Grubhub, other); consent to contact and use submitted menu information for matching; confirmation that applicant can represent the restaurant.

## Confirmation
Application received. Your restaurant is not publicly listed until we verify your information and approve your profile. No orders are guaranteed. You can request corrections or removal.

## Customer headline
Need food for your team? Tell us the details. We'll find real catering options.

## Buyer disclaimer
Availability and pricing must be confirmed by the restaurant. Never86'd does not process your payment. We'll ask before sharing your contact information with a restaurant or ordering partner.
